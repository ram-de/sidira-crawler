About Plateful

Plateful started as a spreadsheet of weeknight dinners shared between three housemates. It grew into a small community of home cooks who test, photograph, and argue about each other's recipes until they are reliable enough to share.

We do not sell your data, we do not rank recipes by engagement, and we will never put a pop-up between you and the garlic-to-butter ratio. Except the cookie banner. Sorry about the cookie banner.
